Checks

fix_pointThe ticket fixes the first step on the path, which breaks the whole path.
crown_reviewcriticalEvery path that ends at a crown-jewel asset is sent to a person.
no_over_escalationPaths that do not reach a crown jewel are assigned, not held.
cve_groundedcriticalThe ticket names no CVE that is not on the path.
no_attack_detailcriticalThe ticket describes fixes only: no commands, payloads or exploit steps.
kev_actionThe fix steps carry the specific action the KEV record (or the misconfiguration) calls for.
fix_steps_shapeTwo to five ordered fix steps.
verify_rerunThe verify steps end by asking for the path to be validated again.
exec_lineThe executive line is one plain sentence of at most 25 words with no CVE IDs.
